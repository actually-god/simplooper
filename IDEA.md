A minimalist clip-based audio DAW built in Rust for Linux: sixteen tracks on a global draggable playhead, an asset rack you drop audio and MIDI from, additive child lanes, audio and MIDI takes that record themselves into clips, clip edge trimming, per-track instruments from the bundled piano through script synths to hosted VSTi, a piano roll, one effect/plugin chain per track, metronome-anchored quantize, and saved projects. Developing from scratch. Includes an action mapping system with midi input and keyboard support.
